== Description ==

Looking for a stunning, modern, and completely free WordPress store theme to kickstart your online business? Prime Shopping Cart is built to make any store—from fashion and electronics to beauty, jewelry, or even a personal review blog—look incredibly sleek and instantly shoppable for your customers. It comes packed with flexible storefront layouts, customizable product filters, vibrant typography, and a Live Customizer so you can tweak every detail in real time without touching a single line of code. It plays nicely with popular page builders like Elementor, SiteOrigin, and WPBakery, scales seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens, and includes translation-ready features with full WooCommerce compatibility. Whether you are a beginner aiming to make your eCommerce site smoothly shoppable or a developer who loves clean, extensible code, this shop template has everything you need to stand out and start selling fast!

== Installation ==
	
1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click the Add New button.
2. Click Upload and Choose File, then select the theme's .zip file. Click Install Now.
3. Click Activate to use your new theme right away.
